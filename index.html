Arctic Map Builder

A free tool for ArcticMUD players. Build maps from your log files, walk them, edit them, and export them for the MUSHclient mapper plugin.

Your data stays with you

Nothing you load here is uploaded anywhere. Logs are read inside your browser and your world is saved in this browser only. Other visitors cannot see or change it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it, so use Save world file now and then. Logs can contain other players' names and chat; they never leave your computer.

The starter maps

This page comes with a set of maps already made (Balifor, Kalaman, Patience, the Brog settlement and others). They are your own copy to edit. New world starts empty, and Load the starter maps brings them back. Room names and descriptions are ArcticMUD's own text.

Quick help

Add logs / maps… (or drop files on the page) · double-click an area tile to open it · click a room to select, shift-click or shift-drag to select several · right-click a room to teleport there · numpad moves you (type help in the game panel).
